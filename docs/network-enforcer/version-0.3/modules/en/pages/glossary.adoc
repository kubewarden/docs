== A

[#_acknowledged-violation]
=== Acknowledged violation

A <<_violation,violation>> that you mark as accepted, with a reason. You add
the annotation `networkenforcer.kubewarden.io/acknowledge-<id>` to the
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>, with the reason as the
value. The `<id>` is the `id` of the <<_violation-record,violation record>>.
The <<_controller,controller>> moves the record to
`status.acknowledgedViolations`, keeps the reason, and removes the
annotation. It also emits a `policy_violation_acknowledged` event.

[#_active-violation]
=== Active violation

A <<_violation-record,violation record>> that is still in
`status.violations`. It is not <<_acknowledged-violation,acknowledged>>. The
field `status.activeViolationCount` holds the number of active violations.
This is the number that needs a decision.

[#_allow-list]
=== Allow-list

The set of <<_peer,peers>>, protocols, and ports that a workload can talk
to, in one <<_direction,direction>>. It is the `spec.kubernetes` or
`spec.istio` payload of a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>> or a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>. A <<_flow,flow>> that is
not in the allow-list is a <<_violation,violation>>.

[#_authorizationpolicy]
=== AuthorizationPolicy

An Istio resource, `security.istio.io/v1`. The
<<_native-policy-object,native policy object>> for the `istio`
<<_backend,backend>>. The <<_controller,controller>> renders one per
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>, with `action: ALLOW`, the
learned <<_principal,principals>>, and the learned ports. In
<<_monitor,monitor>> mode it carries the <<_dry-run,dry-run>> annotation.

== B

[#_backend]
=== Backend

The policy model that a proposal or policy carries. It is the field
`spec.backend`. The values are `istio` and `kubernetes`. The
<<_provider,provider>> decides the backend: the `istio` provider writes the
`istio` backend, the `calico` and `cilium` providers write the `kubernetes`
backend. The field cannot change after creation.

[#_best-effort-monitor]
=== Best-effort monitor

A property of <<_monitor,monitor>> mode. The controller reports a
<<_violation,violation>> only for a <<_flow,flow>> that the
<<_scraper,scraper>> sees. On Istio, ztunnel logs cover inbound TCP through
the mesh only. On Calico and Cilium, the controller compares the same flows
that drive learning. A flow that is not observed is not reported.

== C

[#_calico]
=== Calico

A <<_cni,CNI>>. A <<_provider,provider>> of {ne-short-name}. The
<<_flow-source,flow source>> is <<_goldmane,Goldmane>>. The
<<_backend,backend>> is `kubernetes`. Learning covers TCP and UDP, in both
<<_direction,directions>>.

[#_cert-manager]
=== cert-manager

A prerequisite. Together with the cert-manager CSI driver, it issues the TLS
certificates for the <<_opentelemetry-collector,OpenTelemetry collector>> and
for the OTLP ingestion endpoint of the <<_controller,controller>>.

[#_cilium]
=== Cilium

A <<_cni,CNI>>. A <<_provider,provider>> of {ne-short-name}. The
<<_flow-source,flow source>> is <<_hubble-relay,Hubble Relay>>. The
<<_backend,backend>> is `kubernetes`. Learning covers TCP and UDP, in both
<<_direction,directions>>.

[#_cni]
=== CNI

Container Network Interface. The plugin that connects pods to the network
and enforces Kubernetes <<_networkpolicy,NetworkPolicy>> resources.
{ne-short-name} supports <<_calico,Calico>> and <<_cilium,Cilium>> as
providers. <<_istio-ambient,Istio ambient>> keeps the CNI in place and adds
its own <<_data-plane,data plane>> on top.

[#_controller]
=== Controller

The only component that {ne-short-name} installs, as a Deployment with one
replica. It runs the <<_scraper,scraper>> and four reconcilers. The learning
reconciler writes proposals. The proposal reconciler handles
<<_promotion,promotion>>. The policy reconciler writes the
<<_native-policy-object,native policy object>>. The
<<_status-sync-interval,status sync>> writes violations to the policy status.
The controller also exports events to the
<<_opentelemetry-collector,OpenTelemetry collector>>.

== D

[#_data-plane]
=== Data plane

The layer that forwards traffic between pods and enforces network policy.
{ne-short-name} does not observe traffic on its own. It reads
<<_flow,flows>> from the data plane that already runs in the cluster, and it
writes policy in the format of that data plane. See
<<_provider,provider>>.

[#_direction]
=== Direction

Ingress or egress. Ingress is traffic that a workload receives. Egress is
traffic that a workload sends. Each proposal and policy covers one workload
in one direction. The name ends in `-ingress` or `-egress`. With the
`kubernetes` <<_backend,backend>>, one connection produces an egress
proposal on the source and an ingress proposal on the destination. With the
`istio` backend, learning produces ingress proposals only.

[#_dry-run]
=== Dry-run

The <<_monitor,monitor>> behavior on <<_istio-ambient,Istio ambient>>. The
<<_authorizationpolicy,AuthorizationPolicy>> carries the annotation
`istio.io/dry-run: "true"`. <<_ztunnel,ztunnel>> evaluates the policy and
logs the decision, but does not deny the connection. In
<<_protect,protect>> mode the controller removes the annotation. The
feature needs the istiod flag `AMBIENT_ENABLE_DRY_RUN_AUTHORIZATION_POLICY`.

== E

[#_east-west-traffic]
=== East-west traffic

Traffic between workloads inside the cluster. {ne-short-name} learns and
enforces this traffic only. It does not learn traffic to or from
destinations outside the cluster.

== F

[#_flow]
=== Flow

One observed connection between two workloads: source, destination,
protocol, and port. The <<_scraper,scraper>> turns each flow into a learning
event or a <<_violation,violation>> observation. A flow with one end
outside the cluster, or on a `hostNetwork` pod, is dropped.

[#_flow-dumper]
=== Flow dumper

An optional debug endpoint of the <<_controller,controller>>. The
<<_scraper,scraper>> writes each <<_flow,flow>> to a ring buffer. A `GET
/flow` request drains the buffer and returns the flows as JSON lines. It is
off by default. Enable it with the Helm value `controller.flowDumper.enabled`.

[#_flow-source]
=== Flow source

The service that the <<_scraper,scraper>> reads <<_flow,flows>> from. It
depends on the <<_provider,provider>>: <<_ztunnel,ztunnel>> logs for Istio
ambient, <<_goldmane,Goldmane>> for Calico, <<_hubble-relay,Hubble Relay>>
for Cilium.

[#_fluent-bit]
=== fluent-bit

A log forwarder. With the `istio` <<_provider,provider>>, the Helm chart
deploys it as the `istio-fluent-bit` DaemonSet. It reads the
<<_ztunnel,ztunnel>> log files on each node, parses the JSON, and sends the
records to the <<_controller,controller>> as OTLP logs on port 4317.

== G

[#_goldmane]
=== Goldmane

The flow aggregation service of <<_calico,Calico>>. The
<<_flow-source,flow source>> for the `calico` <<_provider,provider>>. The
<<_controller,controller>> reads it over gRPC with mutual TLS. Enable it in
the Tigera `Installation` resource with `goldmane.enabled=true`.

== H

[#_hubble-relay]
=== Hubble Relay

The observability service of <<_cilium,Cilium>>. The <<_flow-source,flow
source>> for the `cilium` <<_provider,provider>>. The
<<_controller,controller>> follows the `GetFlows` stream over gRPC. Enable it
with `hubble.enabled=true` and `hubble.relay.enabled=true`.

== I

[#_istio-ambient]
=== Istio ambient

The sidecar-free mode of Istio. The default <<_provider,provider>> of
{ne-short-name}. The <<_flow-source,flow source>> is the log of
<<_ztunnel,ztunnel>>, forwarded by <<_fluent-bit,fluent-bit>>. The
<<_backend,backend>> is `istio`. Enforcement happens at the destination
ztunnel, inbound, for TCP only. A namespace joins the mesh with the label
`istio.io/dataplane-mode=ambient`. The label must be present before the pods
are created.

== L

[#_learn-phase]
=== Learn phase

The first <<_phase,phase>>. The <<_scraper,scraper>> observes
<<_flow,flows>>. The <<_controller,controller>> writes them to a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>> for each
workload and <<_direction,direction>>. Nothing is blocked. Learning is
cluster-wide and starts when the controller starts. It does not reconstruct
flows from before that moment. Learning stops for a workload and direction
when a <<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>> with the same name
exists.

== M

[#_mode]
=== Mode

The field `spec.mode` of a <<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>.
The values are <<_monitor,`monitor`>> and <<_protect,`protect`>>. The
default is `monitor`. You change the mode with `kubectl edit` or `kubectl
patch`.

[#_monitor]
=== Monitor

A <<_phase,phase>> and a <<_mode,mode>>. The <<_controller,controller>>
reports each <<_flow,flow>> that the policy does not allow as a
<<_violation,violation>>, with `action: monitor`. Nothing is blocked. On
Istio, the <<_authorizationpolicy,AuthorizationPolicy>> runs in
<<_dry-run,dry-run>>. On Calico and Cilium, no
<<_native-policy-object,native policy object>> exists in this mode. See
<<_best-effort-monitor,best-effort monitor>>.

== N

[#_native-policy-object]
=== Native policy object

The resource that the <<_data-plane,data plane>> enforces. For the `istio`
<<_backend,backend>> it is an <<_authorizationpolicy,AuthorizationPolicy>>.
For the `kubernetes` backend it is a <<_networkpolicy,NetworkPolicy>>. The
<<_controller,controller>> creates it with the same name and namespace as
the <<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>, and sets an owner
reference to the policy.

[#_networkpolicy]
=== NetworkPolicy

A Kubernetes resource, `networking.k8s.io/v1`. The
<<_native-policy-object,native policy object>> for the `kubernetes`
<<_backend,backend>>. The <<_cni,CNI>> enforces it. The
<<_controller,controller>> creates it in <<_protect,protect>> mode only, and
deletes it when the mode returns to <<_monitor,monitor>>.

== O

[#_opentelemetry-collector]
=== OpenTelemetry collector

The service that receives the <<_violation,violation>> events from the
<<_controller,controller>>. The Helm value `telemetry.collectorStrategy`
selects it: `default` deploys a collector, `external` points at your own,
`none` turns the export off. The controller emits a
`policy_violation_observed` event for each violation and a
`policy_violation_acknowledged` event for each
<<_acknowledged-violation,acknowledgement>>. The bundled collector exports
the Prometheus counter `network_enforcer_policy_denies`. This export path is
separate from the OTLP ingestion path that <<_fluent-bit,fluent-bit>> uses.

== P

[#_peer]
=== Peer

The other workload of a <<_flow,flow>>. In the `kubernetes`
<<_backend,backend>>, a peer is a `namespaceSelector` and a `podSelector`.
In the `istio` backend, a peer is a <<_principal,principal>>.

[#_phase]
=== Phase

One of the three stages of the {ne-short-name} workflow:
<<_learn-phase,learn>>, <<_monitor,monitor>>, and <<_protect,protect>>.
Monitor and protect are also the two values of the <<_mode,mode>> field.
Learn has no mode. In the learn phase, the workload has a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>> and no
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>.

[#_principal]
=== Principal

The identity of a workload in <<_istio-ambient,Istio ambient>>, in SPIFFE
form: `cluster.local/ns/<namespace>/sa/<serviceaccount>`. It comes from the
ServiceAccount of the pod. Pods without an explicit ServiceAccount use
`default`, so they share one principal. The `istio` <<_backend,backend>>
names <<_peer,peers>> by principal.

[#_promote-label]
=== Promote label

The label `networkenforcer.kubewarden.io/promote` on a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>>. The value
is `monitor` or `protect`. It starts the <<_promotion,promotion>>. A
<<_validating-admission-policy,Validating Admission Policy>> rejects other
values.

[#_promoted-from-label]
=== Promoted-from label

The label `networkenforcer.kubewarden.io/promoted-from` on a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>. Its value is the name of
the proposal that the policy came from. The <<_controller,controller>> sets
it. The controller uses it to delete a proposal that the scraper creates
again after promotion.

[#_promotion]
=== Promotion

The step from a <<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>>
to a <<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>. You set the
<<_promote-label,promote label>> on the proposal. The
<<_controller,controller>> creates the policy with the same name, namespace,
and <<_allow-list,allow-list>>. It sets the <<_mode,mode>> from the label
and the <<_promoted-from-label,promoted-from label>>. Then it deletes the
proposal. Promotion is explicit. {ne-short-name} does not promote on its
own.

[#_protect]
=== Protect

A <<_phase,phase>> and a <<_mode,mode>>. The <<_data-plane,data plane>>
blocks each <<_flow,flow>> that the policy does not allow. The connection
fails with a timeout, a reset, or a refusal. The data plane decides which.
The <<_controller,controller>> records a <<_violation,violation>> with
`action: protect`. On Istio, the <<_dry-run,dry-run>> annotation is removed.
On Calico and Cilium, the controller creates the
<<_networkpolicy,NetworkPolicy>>.

[#_provider]
=== Provider

The <<_data-plane,data plane>> that {ne-short-name} reads from and writes
to. You select it once, at installation, with the Helm value
`controller.provider.name`. The values are `istio` (default), `calico`, and
`cilium`. The provider selects the <<_scraper,scraper>>, the
<<_flow-source,flow source>>, and the <<_backend,backend>>.

== S

[#_scraper]
=== Scraper

The part of the <<_controller,controller>> that reads the
<<_flow-source,flow source>>. One scraper runs, for the selected
<<_provider,provider>>. It turns each <<_flow,flow>> into a learning event
or a <<_violation,violation>> observation. It does not write Kubernetes
objects.

[#_status-sync-interval]
=== Status sync interval

The period at which the <<_controller,controller>> writes the buffered
<<_violation,violation>> observations to the
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>> status. The Helm value is
`controller.wnpStatusUpdateInterval`. The default is 30 seconds. A violation
can take up to one interval to appear in `kubectl`. The
<<_opentelemetry-collector,OpenTelemetry>> events arrive at once.

== V

[#_validating-admission-policy]
=== Validating Admission Policy

A Kubernetes admission rule written in CEL. {ne-short-name} installs two.
One rejects a <<_promote-label,promote label>> whose value is not `monitor`
or `protect`. One rejects a change to the owner reference of a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>>.

[#_violation]
=== Violation

A <<_flow,flow>> that a <<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>
does not allow. In <<_monitor,monitor>> mode the flow passes. In
<<_protect,protect>> mode the <<_data-plane,data plane>> blocks it. In both
modes the <<_controller,controller>> emits an event to the
<<_opentelemetry-collector,OpenTelemetry collector>> and keeps a
<<_violation-record,violation record>>.

[#_violation-record]
=== Violation record

One entry in `status.violations` or `status.acknowledgedViolations` of a
<<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>. It holds the `id`, the
source and destination workloads, the protocol, the destination port, and
the <<_mode,mode>>. It also holds the time of the last occurrence and the
policy that denied the flow. Each list holds the 100 most recent records.
The field `status.violationCount` counts all the observations that the
policy ever had. On Istio, the destination port is always 0.

== W

[#_workload]
=== Workload

A Deployment, StatefulSet, or DaemonSet. The <<_learn-phase,learn phase>>
creates one <<_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal,WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal>>
per workload and <<_direction,direction>>. Pods with other owners, and pods
with `hostNetwork: true`, are not workloads for learning.

[#_workloadnetworkpolicy]
=== WorkloadNetworkPolicy

The resource that holds the enforced <<_allow-list,allow-list>> of one
<<_workload,workload>> in one <<_direction,direction>>, plus its
<<_mode,mode>>. Short name `wnp`. Namespaced. The <<_controller,controller>>
creates it on <<_promotion,promotion>>. You can also write one by hand. The
controller renders it to a <<_native-policy-object,native policy object>>.
Its status holds the <<_violation-record,violation records>>. If you delete
it, learning starts again for that workload and direction.

[#_workloadnetworkpolicyproposal]
=== WorkloadNetworkPolicyProposal

The resource that holds the learned <<_allow-list,allow-list>> of one
<<_workload,workload>> in one <<_direction,direction>>. Short name `wnpp`.
Namespaced. The <<_controller,controller>> creates it during the
<<_learn-phase,learn phase>>. Its name is the kind, the name, and the
direction of the workload, for example `deployment-nginx-ingress`. It is
owned by the workload, so Kubernetes deletes it with the workload. Nothing
is enforced while a workload has only a proposal. <<_promotion,Promotion>>
replaces it with a <<_workloadnetworkpolicy,WorkloadNetworkPolicy>>.

== Z

[#_ztunnel]
=== ztunnel

The per-node proxy of <<_istio-ambient,Istio ambient>>. It handles the
Layer 4 traffic of the pods on its node and enforces
<<_authorizationpolicy,AuthorizationPolicy>> resources on inbound
connections. Its JSON log is the <<_flow-source,flow source>> for the
`istio` <<_provider,provider>>. UDP does not pass through ztunnel.
